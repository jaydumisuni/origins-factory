## 1. Authority and anti-drift rule

This document is the canonical Origins Factory product and architecture authority.

Before proposing, implementing, reviewing, or continuing Origins work, an AI, chat, developer, or agent must read:

1. `AI_HANDOFF.md`;

4. `docs/HOST_HP_290_G4.md` when host, storage, OS, packaging, or performance assumptions matter;
5. the current source, tests, branches, issues, and pull requests;
6. the canonical repositories named by the capability being changed.

No participant may silently redefine Origins Factory as:

- the custom operating system;
- a VS Code clone;
- an editor with an AI sidebar;
- a dashboard that merely launches applications;
- a thin Tauri wrapper;
- a model router;
- a coding-agent frontend;
- a duplicate of Hunter, AgentOps, CodeOps, Sergeant, Oracle, Lumi, X-Ray, Ptah, or any specialist Gateway.

Do not create another master plan because a new conversation begins. Update this document only when the owner accepts a genuine product or architecture change. Implementation status belongs in `CURRENT_STATE.md`; detailed component contracts belong with their owning repositories.

When current evidence conflicts with this plan, report the contradiction. Do not silently overwrite accepted intent, and do not claim implementation merely because the architecture is accepted.

## 2. Executive definition

> **Origins Factory is a model-optional, evidence-native mission operating environment that combines durable work ownership, full-stack engineering, cyber-physical control, independent verification, cross-machine continuity, and controlled capability synthesis.**

Origins Factory is the portable workspace the owner opens to work with Hunter. It runs independently on Windows and Linux, later macOS, and is also the primary workspace inside the future custom THETECHGUY/Hunter operating system.

Origins is not defined by comparison with VS Code, Cursor, Codex, or ChatGPT Work. Those products illuminate useful surfaces, but Origins belongs to a broader category:

```text
intent
→ recover the complete mission world
→ compile a bounded capability formation
→ execute through persistent mechanical systems
→ verify independently
→ preserve lineage and recovery
→ improve missing capabilities under governance
→ resume the original mission
```

Code is one surface. A mission may also contain conversations, documents, repositories, terminals, browsers, downloads, applications, models, Nodes, physical devices, firmware, evidence, decisions, failures, handovers, releases, and business operations.

## 3. Why Origins exists

The Huawei P30 Pro/VOG recovery case proved the problem.

That successful mission required a connected world of:

- owner reasoning and decisions;
- GPT conversation and causal analysis;
- Codex/repository engineering;
- TTG Device X-Ray live evidence;
- firmware and local files on Athena;
- GitHub branches, commits, plans, and implementation;
- a Google Drive recovery handover;
- the physical Huawei device;
- repeated correction, testing, and verification;
- deterministic architecture extracted from the successful work.

Without the handover and evidence lineage, the Huawei tool would not have inherited the full understanding that produced it. Origins makes that continuity native instead of manually reconstructed.

The primary value is therefore not autocomplete or chat. It is preservation and execution of the complete lineage of understanding:

```text
why the mission exists
→ what is already known
→ what failed and why
→ what evidence is authoritative
→ what repositories, files, machines, applications, and devices matter
→ what work is active
→ what authority each system holds
→ what the exact safe continuation point is
→ what proves completion
```

## 5. Three independent truths

Origins must never collapse planning, execution, and verification into one optimistic status.

### 5.1 Semantic truth

Hunter and AgentOps own the semantic mission layer:

- owner intent;
- mission purpose;
- accepted decisions;
- task decomposition;
- approvals;
- blockers;
- priorities;
- expected outputs;
- correction loops;
- owner acceptance.

A model may help Hunter interpret and propose. A model does not become the canonical owner of the Workspace or Operation.

### 5.2 Mechanical truth

Origins native services, specialist Gateways, and later Ptah own mechanical execution records:

- exact Node and Provider Generation;
- exact process, terminal, browser, application, or device session;
- inputs, revisions, commands, effects, and timestamps;
- Attempts, partial outputs, failures, cancellations, and retries;
- Artifacts and content digests;
- leases, fences, permissions, and receipts;
- observed postconditions and recovery state.

A client acknowledgement is not completion. A command exit code is not a domain verdict.

### 5.3 Assurance truth

Independent systems own assurance:

- Sergeant for engineering review and release challenge;
- X-Ray for read-first and post-operation device evidence;
- deterministic tests, scanners, contract validators, and proof suites;
- specialist governors for domain policy;
- the human owner for final acceptance where required.

Origins displays and links assurance results. It does not rewrite a `PASS`, `NEEDS WORK`, `BLOCK`, `CERTIFIED`, `INVESTIGATE`, or `UNSAFE` verdict into a more convenient status.

## 6. Canonical mission model

### Workspace

A persistent namespace binding mission purpose, repositories, conversations, Operations, Sessions, capabilities, Nodes, Objects, Artifacts, evidence, decisions, and recovery references.

### Mission

The owner-level objective and acceptance criteria. A Mission may span repositories, machines, applications, devices, and many Operations.

### AgentOps Operation

Durable semantic work identity with state, approvals, parent/child relationships, Attempts, blockers, evidence bindings, correction loops, resume state, and sanitation state.

### Activity

A mechanically independent unit of durable work. Origins may use an interim native representation and later map to Ptah Activity contracts without changing the user-facing mission model.

### Attempt

One physical try to perform an Operation or Activity. Retry creates a new Attempt. Failed Attempts remain visible.

### Session

A recoverable human or software interaction over a Workspace: conversation, editor, terminal, browser, application, model, remote view, or specialist Gateway session.

### Capability

A versioned, discoverable unit of ability with effects, permissions, compatibility, maturity, health, limitations, evidence requirements, cost, privacy class, and failure behavior.

### Node

A physical or virtual machine contributing exact capabilities, operating system, architecture, resources, storage, devices, toolchains, health, and load.

### Object, Revision, View, and Artifact

- **Object:** logical item and role;
- **Revision:** immutable version;
- **View:** derived inspection or projection with explicit limits;
- **Artifact:** promoted durable result such as a patch, package, firmware derivative, proof bundle, report, binary, document, or handover.

### Evidence and Receipt

Producer records supporting bounded claims. Evidence does not automatically equal acceptance.

### Twin

A typed representation of observed state at a known time. Device Twins are proven in Huawei; Origins may later expose Project, Node, Application, Build, or Storage Twins where exact contracts justify them.

### Mission Handover

A portable recovery projection containing references to canonical records, not a second mutable database.

It may include:

- purpose and accepted intent;
- canonical repositories, branches, commits, and documents;
- completed work and failed Attempts;
- active Operations and Sessions;
- Artifacts, digests, and custody locations;
- Nodes, applications, devices, Gateway sessions, and X-Ray evidence;
- decisions, contradictions, blockers, and limitations;
- exact next safe action and continuation conditions.

## 8. Permanent authority boundaries

### Hunter

Owns conversation, context compilation, memory selection, reasoning, planning, capability selection, model routing, reconciliation, contradiction detection, and upgrade proposals.

Hunter does not manufacture mechanical completion, specialist write authority, or independent review verdicts.

### Hunter AgentOps

Owns durable semantic Operation lifecycle, approvals, modes, specialists, parent/child work, evidence transport, correction loops, completion state, and lessons.

AgentOps does not become the execution backend or domain source of truth.

### Hunter CodeOps

Owns repository recovery, project-rule recovery, architecture analysis, bounded coding, structured patches, controlled application, build/test proof, correction, rollback, cross-repository engineering, and Sergeant handoff.

CodeOps cannot approve its own non-trivial work.

### Sergeant

Owns independent engineering review and challenge. It is model-free by default, may optionally use models beneath its own evidence system, and returns canonical review outcomes.

Origins must support Sergeant at architecture challenge, candidate review, and final release proof stages.

### Oracle

Owns retained browser sessions and authorised browser/OS perception and control. The control hierarchy is:

```text
semantic API
→ structured application/accessibility control
→ visual and keyboard/mouse fallback
→ human takeover
```

Oracle may later provide remote-view, file transfer, relay, and forwarding through reviewed transport contracts.

### Lumi DM

Owns downloads, queues, resume, source records, integrity verification, and download history. Oracle may discover a source; Lumi acquires it.

### TTG Device X-Ray and specialist X-Rays

Own read-first evidence, physical-device correlation, diagnosis, prediction, certification, recommendations, post-operation verification, and sealed evidence. X-Ray never grants write authority.

### Specialist Gateways

Own persistent physical-device and domain Operation Sessions, endpoint observations, typed journal truth, provider/worker supervision, deterministic policy, leases, bounded execution, crash recovery, and exact recovery plans.

### Software Builder

Owns productization: dependency preparation, clean packaging workspaces, installers, platform packages, signing, release artifacts, hashes, release publishing, build cleanup, and packaging failure evidence.

### Ptah Space

Is the future independent mechanical Workspace and execution substrate. Ptah provides persistent Workspaces, Activities, Facilities, Providers, Nodes, Objects, Revisions, Artifacts, Grants, Leases, Fences, Receipts, scheduling, recovery, and replaceable execution machinery.

Ptah runtime is not yet authorized. Origins may consume frozen Ptah vocabulary and design replaceable adapters, but must not claim Ptah runtime implementation or secretly rebuild Ptah under another name.

### Specialist applications

Retain approved domain interfaces and deterministic behavior. Origins may launch, attach, embed an authorised view, or display operation projections without copying their engines or policy.

## 10. Capability Compiler

Origins does not merely choose a model. It compiles a Mission against the available capability graph.

Each capability must be discoverable through a versioned manifest containing at least:

```yaml
capability:
  id: codeops.cross_repo_transaction
  version: 1.0.0
  owner: hunter-codeops

  effects:
    - observe
    - draft
    - mutate
    - verify

  compatibility:
    operating_systems: [linux, windows]
    architectures: [x86_64]
    languages: [python, rust, typescript]

  permissions:
    - repository_read
    - repository_write
    - process_execute

  accepts:
    - operation_packet
    - repository_revision

  produces:
    - patch_artifact
    - proof_bundle
    - receipt

  maturity: proven
  review_required: true
  rollback_supported: true
  model_dependency: optional
  failure_behavior: fail_closed
```

The real manifest also records:

- exact Provider and Generation;
- health and readiness;
- known limitations;
- expected evidence;
- effect class (`observe`, `draft`, `simulate`, `mutate`, `publish`, `destructive`, `external_side_effect`);
- resource requirements;
- Node placement constraints;
- privacy and data-egress class;
- cost/usage policy;
- historical proof and failure evidence;
- replacement and rollback route.

Compilation inputs:

```text
Mission objective
+ accepted authority
+ required effects
+ project rules
+ available capabilities
+ available Nodes and Providers
+ model choices
+ privacy, cost, time, and risk constraints
+ expected proof
= bounded worker/capability formation
```

The formation must be the smallest effective loadout, not every available tool or model.

## 11. Controlled capability synthesis

Origins may become stronger through real work, but never through blind self-modification.

A capability gap is confirmed only when exact evidence shows that the required effect cannot be completed safely by the current registered capabilities.

```text
Mission Attempt
→ expected capability/effect
→ actual manifest and limitations
→ observed result or bounded refusal
→ evidence-backed gap diagnosis
→ CAPABILITY_GAP_CONFIRMED
```

Then:

```text
confirmed gap
→ Hunter explains impact and alternatives
→ AgentOps creates a bounded child upgrade Operation
→ owner approval where required
→ CodeOps implements the candidate capability
→ deterministic proof
→ Sergeant independent review
→ controlled canary against the mission that exposed the gap
→ owner/application promotion decision
→ new Generation activated with rollback
→ original Mission resumes
```

Rules:

- the model does not decide alone that a capability is missing;
- the affected capability cannot approve its own upgrade;
- CodeOps cannot approve its implementation;
- Sergeant review does not silently activate the capability;
- promotion requires exact evidence and accepted authority;
- failed candidates and canaries remain visible;
- old Generations remain available for rollback where supported;
- no upgrade expands destructive or external authority implicitly;
- the original Mission must retain its exact pre-upgrade state and resume point.

Huawei’s `KnowledgeGap` lifecycle is the first domain-specific proof:

```text
observed
→ questioned
→ candidate
→ replay_supported
→ hardware_supported
→ specialist_approved
→ ttg_promoted
```

## 12. Runtime architecture

Origins uses three primary implementation planes.

### 12.1 React + TypeScript workspace plane

Accepted responsibility:

- Hunter conversation;
- Factory and Operation views;
- repository explorer, editor, search, Git, and diff surfaces;
- terminals and process views;
- Oracle, Lumi, X-Ray, Sergeant, applications, devices, storage, and recovery surfaces;
- approvals, authority, takeover, and visible limitations;
- Armory and Evolution views;
- cross-client layout and status projections.

React and TypeScript are accepted for the workspace UI plane. The exact editor, terminal, docking, desktop host, and rendering libraries remain implementation decisions subject to evidence and licensing review.

### 12.2 Origins native core — Rust

A persistent native service, provisionally referred to as `originsd`, owns local mechanical coordination:

- process and PTY supervision;
- filesystem and Git observation/authority;
- native application discovery and launching;
- local Session attachment;
- Node capability and resource discovery;
- capability registry and health;
- secure IPC and secret references;
- high-throughput event transport;
- cancellation, resource limits, and worker supervision;
- crash recovery and reconnect-safe state;
- local Artifact/content handling;
- specialist Gateway and later Ptah Provider attachment.

The UI must not own durable runtime truth.

```text
Origins UI closes
→ originsd remains alive
→ approved durable work continues
→ Sessions and Attempts remain recorded
→ UI reconnects to exact state
```

### 12.3 Hunter intelligence plane — Python

Python owns or mounts:

- Hunter integration;
- AgentOps;
- CodeOps;
- model/provider adapters;
- context compilation;
- mission planning and reconciliation;
- capability selection and gap diagnosis;
- repository/evidence intelligence;
- specialist adapters and reconnect-safe clients;
- learning proposals and handover generation.

Python intelligence is replaceable and restartable. It must not become the only holder of mechanical state.

### 12.4 Contracts

Rust, Python, and TypeScript communicate through versioned typed contracts. Schema generation or equivalent cross-language validation must preserve:

- canonical identity;
- enum-safe states;
- exact timestamps and revisions;
- immutable evidence metadata;
- recursive secret redaction;
- failure and partial-result states;
- compatibility and migration rules;
- deterministic serialization/digests where required.

Huawei’s Python/Rust typed-contract and canonical-JSON work is the primary donor. A new protocol must not be invented without reviewing that implementation.

### 12.5 Desktop packaging

Tauri may be selected as the desktop packaging/native-window layer, but Tauri is not the Origins runtime architecture. The persistent Rust service must remain independently testable and operable.

## 16. Ptah relationship

Origins is the human/Hunter-facing mission environment. Ptah is the future neutral world and machinery in which durable Activities may run.

Origins may use an interim native runtime before Ptah authorization, but it must preserve replaceable boundaries:

```text
Origins Mission and AgentOps Operation
→ Facility request
→ interim Origins native Provider OR future Ptah Provider
→ Receipt, Events, Artifacts, and recovery state
```

Origins should adopt compatible concepts now where they are already frozen:

- Workspace and Session separation;
- Activity, Operation, and Attempt separation;
- Node, Facility, Provider, and Generation;
- Object, Content, Revision, View, and Artifact;
- Grant, Lease, and Fence;
- effect classes;
- external-reference/materialization states;
- partial results and stable handles;
- exact preconditions and postcondition proof;
- reconnect, recovery, and replacement.

Origins must not block its first useful release waiting for Ptah, and it must not undermine Ptah by embedding permanent one-off mechanical truth into the UI or Python layer.

## 17. Storage, sanitation, and provenance

Core rules:

- one canonical owner per durable fact;
- references preferred over copies;
- exact bytes deduplicated only within authorised privacy/security scope;
- Git owns source and versioned engineering documents;
- active build work uses Node-local storage;
- durable object storage holds immutable Artifacts and evidence;
- Drive holds readable recovery copies and handovers, not live Git/build/runtime databases;
- failed and partial Attempts remain visible;
- temporary state is isolated by Workspace, Operation/Activity, and Attempt;
- accepted outputs are promoted explicitly;
- caches and disposable derived data are labelled;
- no completion without sanitation status;
- no deletion without authority, provenance, retention, and recovery review.

Every substantial Mission should be able to produce a sanitation receipt describing:

- retained canonical records;
- promoted Artifacts;
- failed/partial evidence retained;
- temporary work removed;
- caches retained or purged;
- duplicate or superseded material identified;
- unresolved storage pressure or missing backups.

- VPS/rented workers;
- Cloudflare, Fireworks, and other hosted providers;
- future mini-PC and GPU Nodes;

Workspace and AgentOps identities remain stable while work moves. Mechanical Session truth remains with the Node/Gateway/Provider that owns it.

The confirmed HP 290 G4 host is recorded in `docs/HOST_HP_290_G4.md`. It is capable of running the first complete system and coordinating remote compute. No major RAM upgrade is required to begin Origins development.

## 20. Security and authority

- least privilege applies to humans, models, capabilities, Providers, and Nodes;
- secrets are references, never chat content, logs, committed files, or model context;
- data egress is explicit per model/provider/capability;
- observe, draft, simulate, mutate, publish, destructive, and external-side-effect operations remain distinct;
- external provider permission, Origins/Ptah Grant, and owner approval are separate boundaries;
- protected actions require exact target identity and authority;
- device actions additionally require specialist policy, leases, immutable pre-evidence, bounded execution, and independent read-back verification;
- no model may type passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, payment data, or similar secrets without explicit secure human-controlled design;
- remote control is visible and revocable;
- donor source requires security, licensing, provenance, and credential audits;
- public repositories do not receive private customer, device, payment, employee, or restricted operational data.

## 24. Explicit non-goals

Origins does not:

- replace every specialist repository;
- absorb the custom OS;
- let one model own work state;
- treat chat memory as canonical engineering truth;
- let CodeOps review itself;
- let Sergeant mutate or merge implementation automatically;
- let X-Ray write devices;
- let Hunter bypass specialist governors or leases;
- equate test execution with architecture discovery;
- copy all project data into one repository;
- use Drive as a live build filesystem;
- promise autonomous self-improvement without owner/reviewer gates;
- claim Ptah runtime before authorization;
- inherit donor credentials, policies, or unsafe assumptions.

## 25. Change control

A product/architecture change requires:

1. recovered evidence;
2. explicit contradiction or new requirement;
3. impact analysis across authorities and repositories;
4. owner acceptance;
5. update to this document in place;
6. update to `AI_HANDOFF.md` and `CURRENT_STATE.md` where necessary;
7. independent review before implementation when the change is substantial.

Implementation details that do not alter accepted product intent belong in ADRs, contracts, source, tests, and component documentation—not by rewriting this plan.

Origins Factory is the environment from which missions, applications, engineering systems, device tools, and new capabilities can be built, operated, challenged, recovered, and evolved.
